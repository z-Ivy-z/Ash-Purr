# Ash & Purr
## Video Game

**By:** Ian Rommel Moreno Angeles and Ivana Mariel Moreno Angeles

# Synopsis

You are a small knight whose most treasured possession has been taken away: Almohadita, his inseparable feline companion. Determined to rescue him, you embark on a dangerous odyssey through a kingdom consumed by mystery and corruption.

Along your journey, you will face powerful champions, legendary knights, colossal beasts, and creatures that defy all comprehension. However, the closer you get to your goal, the more you will discover that Almohadita's kidnapping is only the beginning of a much darker conspiracy, one capable of deciding the fate of the entire kingdom. Your journey will not only test your combat skills, but also uncover the truth behind the cat you swore to protect.

# Mechanics

An arcade-style action game with combat inspired by souls-like games, focused exclusively on boss battles. Each encounter takes place in a closed arena where you must study the enemy's attack patterns, dodge with precision, and find the perfect moment to counterattack.

Before each battle, you may equip a single charm to adapt your strategy. However, you only have one life to complete the entire adventure. If you fall against any boss, you must restart the game from the very first battle, putting your skill, patience, and ability to master every encounter to the test.

# Gameplay

The core gameplay revolves around dodging, analyzing attack patterns, and striking at the perfect moment, as most opponents are creatures and warriors far larger and more powerful than the knight.

You will wield a primary weapon that allows you to perform basic attacks and agile movements characteristic of a 2D action game, combining speed, precision, and positioning throughout combat.

You will have access to a charm system that grants unique advantages and disadvantages, allowing you to adapt your playstyle before each encounter.

You will also have access to various support tools that can assist you during battles. However, you must choose them carefully, as each boss has different strengths and weaknesses, making certain tools more effective than others depending on the fight.

# Charm System

Throughout the game, different charms appear that do not belong to the Almohadita from your time or location, as if your Almohadita had sent them to help you rescue him. You know they are not from your cat, yet there is something strangely familiar about their warmth.

Personal belongings of the knight that once belonged to Almohadita. Before each battle, the player may choose a single charm to equip. Each one grants an advantage, but also a disadvantage, forcing the player to adapt their strategy for every boss.

## 1. Almohadita's Photograph

### Description

A small photograph, already worn by the passage of time, showing the knight alongside Almohadita before his kidnapping. Looking at it reminds him of the reason for his journey, filling him with unwavering determination, though he neglects his own safety.

### Effect

⬆️ Increases attack power.  
⬇️ Increases damage received.

## 2. Almohadita's Collar

### Description

The old collar that Almohadita always wore. Keeping it close gives the knight a feeling of calm and protection, allowing him to withstand attacks more effectively, although it makes him more cautious when using his tools.

### Effect

⬆️ Increases defense.  
⬇️ Increases tool cooldown time.

## 3. Fish-Shaped Plush Toy

### Description

Almohadita's favorite toy, kept by the knight since he was a kitten. Carrying it gives him the strength to endure longer in battle, although his attachment causes him to fight with less aggression.

### Effect

⬆️ Increases maximum health.  
⬇️ Reduces attack power.

# Tools

Support items that the knight can use during combat to complement the attacks of his flail. Each tool has a cooldown before it can be used again.

## 1. Throwing Knife

### Description

A small steel knife perfectly balanced for precise throwing. It is ideal for striking enemies from a distance or taking advantage of brief attack windows without exposing yourself too much.

### Effect

🗡️ Travels in a straight line.  
🎯 Deals moderate ranged damage.

### Most effective against:

The Uknow One

## 2. Short Sword

### Description

A lightweight sword that the knight keeps as a secondary weapon. Its quick handling allows him to perform a fast strike whenever the enemy leaves an opening.

### Effect

⚔️ Fast melee attack.  
⚡ Activates almost instantly.

### Most effective against:

The Lost Spirit

## 3. Small Mace

### Description

A one-handed mace designed to break the guard of heavily armored enemies. Although slower than the short sword, each strike is devastating.

### Effect

🔨 Deals high damage.  
💥 Can interrupt certain enemy attacks.

### Most effective against:

The White Lion  
Lady Death

## 4. Dreamcatcher

### Description

An ancient dreamcatcher woven with enchanted threads. According to legend, it absorbs part of the pain of its bearer and transforms that energy into a small amount of relief during battle.

### Effect

🩹 Restores a small percentage of the knight's health.  
⏳ Requires a cooldown before it can be used again.

# Arena

Every encounter takes place in a unique arena that reflects the state of the kingdom and the influence of the darkness consuming it. As the tournament progresses, the arenas gradually deteriorate, revealing the effects of the chaos caused by the final boss's plans.

Structures begin to crumble, corruption spreads throughout the environment, and the atmosphere becomes increasingly hostile, representing how The Unknown One's power slowly affects the world as the knight approaches the truth.

---

# Main Characters

## Almohadita

A male cat approximately two years old, with white and gray tuxedo-pattern fur and orange eyes. Before being kidnapped, he was the inseparable companion of the little knight, sharing every adventure with him. His disappearance is the reason that drives the protagonist to travel across the kingdom.

### True Nature

Long before the kingdom was born, an ancient deity sealed an immense source of power capable of restoring life to any land... or condemning it to eternal darkness. To prevent that power from falling into the wrong hands, it was hidden inside an innocent living vessel that would go unnoticed by the world: a small cat. As the centuries passed, that vessel eventually became Almohadita, who lived his entire life without knowing the truth about his origin. His presence keeps the seal intact, but if the power he contains were ever released or corrupted, it would have the ability to restore the kingdom... or destroy it forever.

### Power

He possesses the ability to travel through time and space across different dimensions and realities. This power remains hidden within Almohadita, and attempting to release it incorrectly is what causes the distortion and collision of realities. He can use this power to send you charms that belonged to different Almohaditas throughout time in order to help you reach him.

---

## Bob

A small knight wearing medieval armor who never removes his small helmet. No one knows his true appearance; where his eyes should be, only a deep dark shadow can be seen. Agile and determined, he fights using a small tree branch with a single leaf as his primary weapon. Among his few treasured possessions, he carries a photograph of Almohadita along with several belongings that once belonged to the cat, memories that give him the strength to keep fighting and rescue him.

An ancient mage whose spirit never found peace after death and was summoned once again, bound by a dark force with the sole purpose of stopping your advance. He silently floats above the ground, surrounded by a faint spectral aura, attacking from a distance with energy born from the magic he once swore to use to protect the innocent. Although he fights without hesitation, deep within his gaze still remains the echo of a condemned hero who never found rest.

# Lady Death

## Description

An elite assassin hired by the king to eliminate anyone attempting to approach the cat. Cold and calculating, she serves only the highest bidder and harbors a particular hatred toward cats, considering them creatures that bring misfortune. Her speed and precision make her one of the deadliest opponents in the kingdom.

## Difficulty

★★★☆☆

## Attacks

#### 🔹 Twin Slash

Performs a rapid combination of slashes using her twin daggers.

#### 🔹 Shadow Step

Disappears for an instant before reappearing behind the player to execute a surprise attack.

#### 🔹 Throwing Daggers

Throws three daggers in rapid succession from a distance.

---

## Second Phase (50% Health)

Lady Death abandons caution and fights with greater aggression.

Twin Slash finishes with a spinning slash that increases its range.

Throwing Daggers launches five daggers in a fan pattern.

Shadow Step can be chained twice before stopping, forcing the player to react quickly.

This battle is designed to be one of the fastest in the game, rewarding reflexes, patience, and proper dodge timing.

# The White Lion

## Description

A massive feline beast that grew deeply attached to the little cat and eventually came to consider him its own cub. Since then, it has protected him with unmatched ferocity and will not allow anyone to take him away, convinced that it alone is capable of keeping him safe.

---

## Difficulty

★★★☆☆

---

## Attacks

#### 🔹 Savage Pounce

Leaps toward the player from a long distance, attempting to crush them with its full weight.

#### 🔹 Claw Combo

Performs a rapid combination of three consecutive claw strikes.

#### 🔹 Roar

Lets out a powerful roar that creates a shockwave around itself, pushing the player away if they are too close.

---

## Second Phase (50% Health)

The White Lion enters a state of fury to protect its "cub."

Savage Pounce is performed twice in succession.

Claw Combo gains a fourth finishing claw strike with greater range.

Roar has a larger area of effect and is immediately followed by a charge attack.

This battle focuses on dodging fast attacks and taking advantage of the brief moments when the lion is left vulnerable after its leaps and charges. Unlike other bosses, it does not fight out of malice, but out of a protective instinct, making it one of the most tragic enemies in the game.

# The Cat Keeper (Penultimate Boss)

## Style

Simple 2D with solid colors. Shadows should also use solid colors, creating a cute and pleasant art style with well-defined shapes.

---

## Story

A great knight, recognized as the kingdom's greatest warrior and the king's most loyal servant. Throughout the entire game, he has protected the cat, convinced that setting it free would bring back an ancient evil. However, he is unaware that the true king was replaced by The Unknown One, who manipulated his orders and turned him into the final guardian before reaching the one responsible for the kidnapping. Armed with a colossal sword and flawless discipline, he will fight until the very end to fulfill what he believes is his king's will.

---

## Personality

A large and proud man with unwavering faith and loyalty toward his king. Noble and imposing, he recognizes the determination and incredible strength of will that the little knight has demonstrated in order to reach this point.

---

## Appearance

A strong and vigorous man covered by massive polished silver armor and a heavy black cape. His face is slightly deformed from the countless battles he has fought. He is Caucasian, with black eyes and black hair.

---

## Difficulty

★★★★★

---

## Fighting Style

He fights slowly but powerfully, performing simple yet devastating combos combined with his special attacks, leaving clear but brief openings for the player to counterattack. He has two attack combos:

### Basic Combo

Consists of three attacks, followed by a short opportunity to counterattack before usually performing a special attack.

### Advanced Combo

Consists of five attacks, leaving a slightly longer opening than the Basic Combo. Only rarely is it followed by a special attack.

# Second Phase (40% Health)

The temporal fracture causes the champion's mind to collide with countless versions of himself from entirely different places and eras, driving him into madness as the space-time anomalies merge multiple identities within his consciousness.

**[The arena becomes covered with violent, unpredictable fractures, allowing realities to collide.]**

**Personality:** The knight begins letting out horrifying screams of pain while expressing complete confusion over what is happening inside his mind, eventually losing the ability to speak and producing only agonizing, beast-like sounds.

The Guardian violently tears off his armor and begins fighting in a far more frenzied, savage, and uncontrolled manner.

Assault is performed twice in succession.

Seismic Strike launches a stronger shockwave.

His Swift Slash combo no longer exhausts him, allowing him to continue attacking immediately afterward.

This battle should feel like the ultimate duel of skill: the boss leaves clear openings after every combo, but punishes mistakes quickly, preparing the player for the final confrontation.

# The Unknown One (Final Boss)

## Description

The entity responsible for the kidnapping and the true source of the corruption consuming the kingdom. It never reveals its true face; its body is made of constantly shifting shadows, perfectly imitating the appearance of the king himself in order to manipulate his subjects and spread chaos. Without the slightest remorse, it used this false identity to pit the protagonist against the kingdom's greatest warriors, sacrificing them solely to delay his progress. During the battle, it uses fragments of the abilities of the defeated bosses, mocking their downfall and turning their strength into its own weapon. Upon defeating it, it is revealed that the cat possesses an ancient power capable of restoring or destroying the kingdom, and that the kidnapping was always part of a plan to corrupt that power and rule the world under an eternal kingdom of darkness.

---

## Difficulty

★★★★☆

---

## Attacks

### 🔹 Shadow Blade

Forms a sword of darkness and performs a rapid combo of slashing attacks.

---

### 🔹 Phantom Orb

Fires a sphere of dark energy that travels slowly and explodes upon hitting the ground or a wall.

---

### 🔹 Mimic Strike

Randomly imitates one of the signature attacks of a previously defeated boss (such as a charge, an energy wave, or a projectile), forcing the player to adapt.

---

# Second Phase (Second Health Bar)

The shadows covering its body expand, and the arena grows darker.

Shadow Blade becomes a longer and faster combo.

Phantom Orb fires two spheres in different directions.

Mimic Strike can chain together two attacks from different bosses before leaving an opening for the player to counterattack.

This final battle puts everything learned throughout the game to the test: dodging, recognizing attack patterns, and taking advantage of brief opportunities to strike, making the final encounter feel like the culmination of every previous boss fight.

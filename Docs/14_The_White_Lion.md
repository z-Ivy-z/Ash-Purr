# The White Lion — Boss Design Document

## Overview

The White Lion is the third boss (between Lady Death and The Lost Spirit). A massive beast who adopted Almohadita as his cub. Fights through explosive charges, powerful leaps, and wide melee attacks. Teaches players to bait committal attacks and punish recovery windows.

## Combat Stats

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| HP | 380 |
| Phase 2 Threshold | 50% HP (190) |
| Attack Interval | 1.4s (P1), 0.9s (P2) |
| Pounce Speed | 600 px/s |
| Movement Speed | 140 px/s |

## Attacks

### Heavy Claw (Basic)
- Wide horizontal swipe
- Damage: 14 (P1), 17 (P2)
- Noticeable recovery after

### Savage Pounce
- Lowers body, locks eyes, launches across arena
- Landing shockwave
- Damage: 18
- Long recovery on miss (punish window)
- P2: Can chain 2 pounces

### Claw Combo
- 3 rapid swipes (P1), 4 swipes (P2)
- Each reaches slightly farther
- Damage: 10 per swipe
- Final swipe exposes briefly

### Roar
- Circular shockwave pushes Bob away
- Damage: 8
- Long telegraph
- P2: Larger shockwave + immediate charge after

## Phase 2 (50% HP)
- Double pounce
- 4-hit claw combo
- Roar + follow-up charge
- Shorter recovery
- More aggressive pacing

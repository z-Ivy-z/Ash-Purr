# Items Design Document

## Overview

Items in **Ash & Purr** are designed to complement player skill rather than replace it.

No item should allow the player to bypass learning enemy mechanics or trivialize encounters. Every item encourages thoughtful preparation and strategic decision-making before entering battle.

Items are divided into five categories:

* **Amulets**
* **Tools**

Each category serves a distinct gameplay and narrative purpose.

---

# Design Philosophy

Every item should reinforce one of the game's core pillars:

* Preparation
* Determination
* Meaningful Choice
* Emotional Storytelling
* Skill over Power

Items should never become the primary source of player strength.

Instead, they provide small advantages that encourage different playstyles while preserving the game's difficulty.

---

# Amulets

## Overview

Throughout the adventure, Bob discovers mysterious objects that once belonged to versions of **Almohadita** from other timelines and realities.

Bob immediately realizes these objects do **not** belong to the Almohadita he is trying to rescue.

They are different.

Older.

Slightly worn in unfamiliar ways.

Yet, despite knowing they are not his companion's belongings, every object radiates a familiar warmth that fills him with hope.

It is as though countless versions of Almohadita are silently helping him complete the journey that none of them could finish alone.

Each amulet represents a memory shared between Bob and another version of Almohadita.

These memories strengthen Bob emotionally rather than physically.

---

## Gameplay Rules

* Only **one amulet** may be equipped before each boss battle.
* Amulets cannot be swapped during combat.
* Every amulet provides both an advantage and a disadvantage.
* There is no universally best amulet.
* Players are encouraged to adapt their choice depending on the upcoming encounter.

Preparation is considered part of mastering the game.

---

## Amulet Design Philosophy

Amulets should never provide unconditional power.

Every benefit must introduce a meaningful drawback.

Choosing an amulet should feel like making a tactical decision rather than selecting an obvious upgrade.

# Amulets

---

## Photograph of Almohadita

### Description

A small photograph worn by time.

It shows Bob and Almohadita together before the kidnapping.

Looking at the picture reminds Bob why he continues fighting, filling him with unwavering determination.

However, his overwhelming desire to save his friend causes him to neglect his own safety.

### Effects

**Advantage**

* Increases attack power.

**Drawback**

* Increases damage received.

---

## Almohadita's Collar

### Description

The old collar Almohadita always wore.

Keeping it close gives Bob a comforting sense of peace and protection.

That calm allows him to better endure enemy attacks, but makes him more cautious when using his combat tools.

### Effects

**Advantage**

* Increases defense.

**Drawback**

* Increases tool cooldown duration.

---

## Fish Plush

### Description

Almohadita's favorite toy.

Bob has carried it ever since Almohadita was a kitten.

Its familiar softness gives him the strength to endure longer battles, though his emotional attachment causes him to fight less aggressively.

### Effects

**Advantage**

* Increases maximum health.

**Drawback**

* Reduces attack power.

# Tools

## Overview

Tools are secondary combat equipment that complement Bob's branch.

They provide tactical opportunities but never replace mastery of the combat system.

Every tool has an independent cooldown.

Proper timing is always more valuable than raw damage.

---

## Design Philosophy

Tools should:

* Create opportunities.
* Reward intelligent use.
* Encourage experimentation.
* Never invalidate boss mechanics.

Players who rely exclusively on tools should struggle.

Players who combine them intelligently with precise combat should be rewarded.

---

## Throwing Knife

### Description

A perfectly balanced steel knife designed for accurate throws.

Ideal for exploiting small openings without exposing Bob to unnecessary danger.

### Effects

* Travels in a straight line.
* Deals moderate ranged damage.
* Quick activation.
* Medium cooldown.

### Most Effective Against

* **The Unknown One**

---

## Short Sword

### Description

A lightweight sword carried as Bob's secondary weapon.

Its fast handling allows an immediate strike whenever the enemy briefly lowers their guard.

### Effects

* Extremely fast melee attack.
* Instant activation.
* Short recovery.
* Medium cooldown.

### Most Effective Against

* **The Lost Spirit**

---

## Small Mace

### Description

A compact one-handed mace designed to crush heavy armor and stagger resilient opponents.

Although slower than the short sword, every strike lands with tremendous impact.

### Effects

* High melee damage.
* Can interrupt certain enemy attacks.
* Slow startup.
* Long cooldown.

### Most Effective Against

* **The White Lion**
* **Lady Death**

---

## Dreamcatcher

### Description

An ancient dreamcatcher woven with enchanted threads.

According to legend, it absorbs part of the bearer's suffering and transforms it into a small moment of relief.

### Effects

* Restores a small percentage of Bob's health.
* Long cooldown.
* Does not remove status effects.

---

# Final Item Design Rule

Whenever a new item is added, it should answer one question:

> **Does this item encourage better decision-making without reducing the importance of player skill?**

If the answer is yes, it belongs in **Ash & Purr**.

If it makes combat easier simply by increasing numbers or allowing players to ignore mechanics, it should be redesigned.

# Lore Bible

# The Origin of Everything

Long before kingdoms, before humanity, before history itself, only one reality existed.

This primordial reality was governed by an ancient cosmic entity known simply as The First Keeper.

The First Keeper was not a god of creation nor destruction.

Its only purpose was maintaining the balance between every possible reality.

Past.

Present.

Future.

Every possible timeline existed in harmony because The First Keeper prevented them from colliding.

Reality was stable.

Time flowed correctly.

Life flourished.

# The Primordial Heart

At the center of existence rested an immense source of primordial energy.

It was neither good nor evil.

It simply amplified intention.

In the hands of someone selfless...

It could restore dying lands.

Heal every wound.

Return entire civilizations to life.

But in the hands of someone consumed by ambition...

It could erase worlds.

Rewrite history.

Merge realities.

Destroy existence itself.

The First Keeper understood that no being should ever possess such power.

# The Great Seal

Unable to destroy the Primordial Heart, The First Keeper sealed it forever.

Instead of hiding it inside a temple...

A weapon...

Or a sacred artifact...

The Keeper made an impossible decision.

It sealed the power inside the purest vessel imaginable.

Not because it was strong.

But because it had no desire for power.

A small, innocent cat.

The vessel would pass unnoticed through history.

Generation after generation.

Never ruling.

Never conquering.

Never seeking greatness.

Simply living.

As long as the vessel remained pure...

Reality would remain stable.

# The Eternal Lineage

Centuries became millennia.

The original vessel died peacefully.

Before each death, the seal naturally migrated into another innocent cat.

None of them remembered their predecessors.

None understood their purpose.

Each simply lived an ordinary life.

Unknown to everyone...

Every cat carrying the seal protected all existence.

Almohadita is merely the latest vessel.

# The Birth of the Kingdom

Thousands of years later...

Human civilization emerged.

Kings built cities.

Knights defended them.

The kingdom prospered.

Legends spoke of miraculous harvests...

Unnaturally fertile lands...

And a mysterious blessing that always protected the realm.

Nobody realized those miracles existed because Almohadita unknowingly lived nearby.

The Primordial Heart constantly healed the world simply by remaining sealed.

# The Unknown One

No one knows where he came from.

No records mention his birth.

No kingdom claims him.

No history remembers a time before his existence.

It is as though he simply appeared.

The oldest surviving texts do not describe a man.

They describe a presence.

A shadow.

A curse that quietly spread from one age to the next.

No one knows his true name.

Only the title history gave him.

The Unknown One.

Unlike every other being, he possesses no compassion.

No empathy.

No remorse.

No love.

No purpose beyond his own amusement.

He delights in suffering.

He delights in fear.

He delights in watching hope disappear from the eyes of others.

He does not seek power because he needs it.

He seeks power because denying it to everyone else brings him pleasure.

He does not wish to rule the kingdom.

He wishes to watch it collapse.

He does not desire loyal subjects.

He desires victims.

Where others build, he destroys.

Where others protect, he corrupts.

Where others inspire hope, he leaves only despair.

He understands the existence of countless realities, not because he studied them, but because his very presence erodes the natural laws separating them.

Reality itself recoils from him.

His mere existence weakens the boundaries between worlds.

# The Kidnapping

When The Unknown One discovered the existence of the vessel, he did not see a miracle.

He saw a toy.

A way to inflict suffering on an unimaginable scale.

He kidnapped Almohadita without hesitation.

Not to save the world.

Not to reshape existence.

Not to fulfill a prophecy.

Simply because he could.

The fear of the innocent amused him.

The grief of others entertained him.

Every attempt to force the Primordial Heart from its vessel was driven by cruelty rather than necessity.

He knew the process was destroying reality.

He continued anyway.

Every fracture.

Every collapsed timeline.

Every lost life.

Every kingdom consumed by chaos.

Was nothing more than entertainment to him.

# The False King

The Unknown One murdered the true king.

Then erased every trace of his existence.

Some people remember a funeral.

Others remember an illness.

Some insist there was never another king at all.

Every version is false.

History itself became one of his victims.

He wore the crown not because he desired to govern.

He simply enjoyed watching an entire kingdom unknowingly obey the monster responsible for its destruction.

Every order he gave was designed to create more suffering.

Families were separated.

Heroes died for meaningless causes.

The innocent became enemies.

The loyal were manipulated into committing atrocities.

The Cat Keeper became his greatest puppet.

Not because he was weak...

But because he was honorable.

The Unknown One viewed that honor as something to exploit.

# His True Nature

The Unknown One is not driven by justice.

Not revenge.

Not ideology.

Not grief.

Not destiny.

There is no tragic past to redeem.

No hidden reason behind his actions.

He is evil because he chooses to be.

Every opportunity to show mercy is rejected.

Every chance to stop is ignored.

Every life placed in his hands is treated as disposable.

He is the one being in the kingdom entirely responsible for his own darkness.

Nothing created him.

Nothing corrupted him.

Nothing forced his hand.

He simply enjoys destroying everything beautiful.

# His Goal

The Unknown One has no dream of a better future.

He has no vision for the kingdom.

He does not intend to create a perfect world after destroying the old one.

He wants only one thing.

To watch existence collapse.

To see every reality consume every other reality.

To reduce all history, every memory and every living soul into an endless void where nothing meaningful can ever exist again.

He seeks the Primordial Heart because it is the only force capable of bringing about complete annihilation.

Not to rule over what remains.

But to ensure that nothing remains at all.

# The Champions

Every major boss represents a different response to the kingdom's collapse.

Some still believe they protect the realm.

Some have surrendered.

Some embraced corruption.

Some fight only because they have forgotten how to stop.

None are truly evil.

All are victims.

Except one.

# The Amulets

When reality fractured...

Every possible version of Almohadita became connected.

Although none could consciously control the Primordial Heart...

Their shared desire was identical.

Return home.

Fragments of countless timelines began slipping into Bob's reality.

Objects once belonging to alternate Almohaditas crossed dimensions.

These became the Amulets.

Every amulet comes from another life.

Another friendship.

Another Bob.

Another ending.

Some belonged to worlds where Bob succeeded.

Others...

Where he failed.

# Bob

Bob never learns the complete truth.

He never seeks power.

He never cares about ancient prophecies.

As far as he is concerned...

His best friend has been stolen.

That is enough.

Ironically...

His complete lack of ambition makes him the only person truly worthy of approaching the Primordial Heart.

# After the Ending

The battle is over.

The Unknown One has fallen.

For the first time in countless ages, reality begins to stabilize.

The fractured skies slowly mend.

The impossible landscapes cease shifting.

Time finally begins flowing as it should.

Bob reaches Almohadita at last.

For a brief moment, everything feels exactly as it did before the journey began.

Almohadita gently walks toward him.

He rubs his head against Bob's armor.

Bob kneels without saying a word.

He carefully picks up his best friend and holds him tightly in his arms.

Silence fills the world.

Then the truth becomes clear.

The Primordial Heart cannot simply remain.

The seal has been broken beyond repair.

The countless fractured realities can only become one again if the vessel willingly releases the power it has carried throughout every lifetime.

Without fear...

Without hesitation...

Almohadita understands.

He never speaks.

He never needed to.

He simply looks into Bob's eyes.

Everything he wishes to say is conveyed through that final gaze.

Gratitude.

Love.

Home.

Bob understands.

For the first time since the beginning of the journey, the little knight can do nothing.

His branch cannot help.

His determination cannot change what must happen.

He embraces Almohadita as tightly as he can.

For several long moments...

Neither of them moves.

Finally...

With trembling hands...

Bob lets him go.

Almohadita slowly walks forward.

A gentle light begins emerging from within him.

The Primordial Heart awakens one final time.

Rather than exploding with destructive force, it releases a warm radiance that peacefully spreads across every fractured reality.

The light grows brighter.

The kingdom disappears beneath its glow.

The sky.

The mountains.

The oceans.

Every possible world.

Everything becomes white.

Then...

Silence.

The light slowly fades.

Reality has been restored.

Time flows naturally once again.

The temporal fractures have vanished.

The impossible landscapes are gone.

The kingdom has been saved.

Yet history cannot erase what has already happened.

Castles remain damaged.

Ancient walls still bear the scars of battle.

Collapsed bridges are slowly being rebuilt.

Nature, however, has begun to heal.

Grass grows once more.

Flowers bloom across forgotten fields.

Birds sing again.

Warm sunlight reaches places that had long known only darkness.

Bob quietly walks beyond the ruins.

He stops beneath a large tree overlooking the restored kingdom.

For the first time since the adventure began...

He allows himself to breathe.

He sits beneath the tree in complete silence.

The wind gently moves the grass.

Leaves drift peacefully overhead.

Then...

A tiny sound.

"Miau."

Bob slowly turns his head.

Standing only a few steps away is a tiny orange kitten.

Its fur shines beneath the sunlight.

Its large amber eyes quietly observe the little knight.

The kitten tilts its head with innocent curiosity.

Bob slowly extends one hand.

The tiny cat cautiously approaches.

It sniffs his gauntlet.

Then gently rubs its head against his hand.

Bob cannot help but smile beneath the darkness of his helmet.

He carefully lifts the kitten into his lap.

The little cat immediately curls into a comfortable ball.

Without understanding why...

Something deep within Bob's heart tells him that this small soul feels familiar.

Not the same body.

Not the same memories.

But the same warmth.

The same comfort.

A new beginning.

The kitten remembers nothing.

It knows nothing of ancient gods...

Forgotten kingdoms...

Or collapsing realities.

Yet, somehow...

It knows this tiny knight is someone special.

It closes its eyes.

Begins softly purring.

And peacefully falls asleep upon Bob's legs.

Bob gently strokes the little cat's head.

After a long silence, he quietly says:

> "We have to find you a name, little one."

The camera slowly pulls away.

The little knight remains beneath the tree.

The kitten sleeps peacefully in his lap.

The wind carries flower petals across the restored kingdom.

Fade to black.

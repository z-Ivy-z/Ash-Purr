# 04 Player

# Bob — Player Character Design Document

## Overview

Bob is the playable protagonist of **Ash & Purr**.

He is a tiny royal knight whose greatest strength is not his body, his weapon, or his equipment—it is his unwavering determination.

Throughout the entire game, Bob remains physically weaker than almost every enemy he encounters. He never becomes an unstoppable warrior, and his humble appearance never changes dramatically. Instead, the player's sense of progression comes from mastering the combat system, understanding enemy patterns, and making better decisions.

Bob is designed to embody the central philosophy of the game:

> **Determination over Power.**

Every animation, gameplay mechanic, visual detail, and sound associated with Bob should reinforce this identity.

---

# Character Role

Bob serves as:

* The player's avatar.
* The emotional center of the story.
* The embodiment of perseverance.
* A visual contrast against the gigantic bosses.
* A constant reminder that courage does not require strength.

Players should immediately empathize with him.

He should appear:

* Small.
* Humble.
* Kind.
* Courageous.
* Fragile.
* Determined.

---

# Gameplay Identity

Bob is **not** a traditional RPG hero.

He does not become stronger through levels or equipment.

Instead, he succeeds because the player improves.

His gameplay identity revolves around:

* Precision
* Mobility
* Timing
* Observation
* Patience
* Consistency

Every mechanic should reward player skill rather than character statistics.

---

# Combat Role

Bob excels at:

* Fast movement
* Quick attacks
* Precise dodges
* Positioning
* Reading enemy patterns

He struggles with:

* Low health
* Very short weapon reach
* Small attack area
* Low damage output
* Punishing mistakes

Every encounter should make the player feel vulnerable, but never powerless.

---

# Visual Design

## Art Style

Bob follows the overall artistic direction of the game.

* Cute stylized 2D artwork
* Flat colors
* Flat cel-shaded shadows
* No gradients
* No realistic textures
* Simple geometric forms
* Smooth rounded silhouettes
* High readability at every scale

Every frame should communicate personality before detail.

---

# Silhouette

Bob's silhouette must remain recognizable instantly.

Primary identifying elements:

* Oversized medieval helmet
* Tiny body
* Short cape
* Branch sword with a single leaf
* Large rounded gloves
* Large armored boots

Even in complete darkness, players should recognize Bob immediately.

---

# Body Proportions

Bob intentionally has exaggerated proportions to maximize readability.

* Very short height
* Large head
* Small torso
* Short expressive arms
* Agile short legs
* Slightly oversized hands
* Large armored boots

His proportions should make him feel almost toy-like while remaining believable inside the game's world.

---

# Helmet

Bob never removes his helmet.

The helmet completely defines his identity.

Design characteristics:

* Rounded medieval great helm
* Smooth steel surfaces
* Three narrow vertical visor slits
* No horns
* No wings
* No spikes
* No engravings
* No magical ornaments

The visor reveals only complete darkness.

There are:

* No visible eyes
* No face
* No glowing pupils

The mystery of who Bob truly is should remain unresolved throughout the game.

Players should naturally project themselves beneath the helmet.

---

# Armor

Bob wears the armor of a humble royal knight.

It should appear:

* Well maintained
* Practical
* Functional
* Comfortable
* Durable

It should **not** appear:

* Legendary
* Ornate
* Ceremonial
* Magical

Armor pieces include:

* Rounded helmet
* Large pauldrons
* Smooth silver chestplate
* Crimson knight tunic
* Oversized gauntlets
* Oversized armored boots
* Black leather belt
* Golden square buckle
* Brown leather straps
* Small functional rivets

Every element reinforces simplicity instead of prestige.

---

# Weapon

Bob's weapon is intentionally unimpressive.

It is nothing more than:

**A small tree branch with a single fresh green leaf.**

The branch represents one of the game's strongest symbols:

True strength does not come from weapons.

The weapon should always appear:

* Lightweight
* Humble
* Natural
* Slightly worn

It never transforms into a legendary sword.

It never gains magical effects.

It remains the same branch from beginning to end.

---

# Color Palette

## Primary Colors

* Bright polished silver armor
* Soft crimson-red tunic
* Matte dark gray visor interior
* Black leather belt

## Secondary Colors

* Warm golden buckle
* Brown leather straps
* Dark brown tree branch
* Bright green leaf

## Accent Colors

Small gold details on:

* Belt
* Armor joints
* Sword belt

The overall palette should remain warm, soft, and inviting.

---

# Animation Philosophy

Bob's animations prioritize personality over realism.

Every movement should communicate determination.

Nothing should feel exaggerated or comedic.

---

# Idle Animation

Bob remains alert even while standing still.

Behavior:

* Gentle breathing
* Slight weight shifting
* Occasionally adjusts his grip
* Looks toward nearby enemies

He never appears relaxed.

He is always prepared.

---

# Running

Bob's run should feel:

* Fast
* Responsive
* Energetic
* Purposeful

His short legs move quickly while maintaining clean, readable animation.

Movement should immediately communicate agility.

---

# Attacking

Attacks are:

* Quick
* Clean
* Efficient

No unnecessary flourishes.

No acrobatics.

Every swing is economical.

Each attack should produce:

* Brief hit stop
* Small screen shake
* Sharp impact effect
* Metallic sound
* Fast recovery

Players should instantly feel every successful hit.

---

# Dodging

The dodge is Bob's defining defensive ability.

Characteristics:

* Fast startup
* Clean animation
* Short duration
* Precise invulnerability frames
* Immediate recovery

A successful dodge should feel satisfying rather than flashy.

---

# Victory Animation

Bob celebrates quietly.

Sequence:

* Raises his branch
* Looks upward
* Gives a small confident nod

He never behaves arrogantly.

Victory is treated as another step toward rescuing Almohadita.

---

# Death Animation

Bob falls slowly.

His branch slips from his hand.

His helmet remains facing forward.

There is no dramatic pose.

His defeat should feel tragic without becoming graphic.

---

# Personality Through Animation

Because Bob's face is never visible, all emotions are communicated through:

* Body language
* Helmet angle
* Timing
* Posture
* Movement speed
* Camera framing

Players should always understand what Bob feels without ever seeing his face.

---

# Emotional Details

Attached to Bob's belt is a small leather pouch.

Inside are his most treasured possessions:

* A worn photograph of Almohadita.
* One of Almohadita's favorite toys.
* Small keepsakes collected throughout their life together.

These items rarely appear during gameplay.

Instead, they are reserved for important narrative moments.

They represent Bob's emotional motivation.

---

# Gameplay Statistics Philosophy

Bob intentionally remains vulnerable throughout the game.

General characteristics:

* Low health
* Low damage
* High mobility
* High responsiveness
* Fast attacks
* Short attack range
* High precision

The player should never feel overpowered.

Success always comes from execution.

---

# Scaling Philosophy

Bob should appear tiny beside every major enemy.

General scale guidelines:

* Regular enemies: approximately 1.5–2× Bob's height.
* Elite enemies: approximately 2–3× Bob's height.
* Bosses: approximately 3–5× Bob's height.

This constant difference in scale reinforces the game's central fantasy.

---

# Audio Identity

Bob's sounds should communicate lightness.

Examples:

* Quiet footsteps.
* Soft armor movement.
* Light breathing.
* Crisp branch swings.
* Sharp dodge sounds.

Compared to bosses, Bob should sound almost silent.

This reinforces his small physical presence.

---

# Camera Behavior

The camera subtly emphasizes Bob's vulnerability.

During gameplay:

* Bob remains slightly below center screen.
* The camera prioritizes visibility over cinematic framing.
* Boss attacks may briefly pull the camera outward to reveal incoming danger.
* Small camera shake accompanies Bob's successful attacks without reducing readability.

---

# Narrative Role

Bob begins the adventure believing he is simply rescuing his best friend.

As the story unfolds, he learns:

* The kingdom is collapsing.
* Reality itself is breaking.
* Almohadita is the vessel sealing a primordial force.

Despite these revelations, Bob's motivation never changes.

He never seeks glory.

He never seeks power.

He never tries to become a hero.

He simply refuses to abandon the friend who never abandoned him.

That unwavering simplicity is what ultimately makes him worthy of deciding the fate of every reality.

---

# Character Design Rules

Every decision involving Bob should follow these principles:

* Keep him visually small.
* Never make him intimidating.
* Never make him physically dominant.
* Prioritize responsiveness over spectacle.
* Emphasize determination in every animation.
* Preserve the mystery of his hidden face.
* Keep his branch simple.
* Let player mastery—not character power—drive progression.

If a new mechanic, animation, ability, or visual change makes Bob feel like a legendary warrior instead of a tiny knight overcoming impossible odds, it should be rejected.
